Questions that help you choose

The right reader depends on how, where, and what you read. These questions are a good place to begin.

Where will you read?

At home in a favorite chair, on a commute, or while travelling. Think about size and how it feels in your hand.

What will you read?

Novels, comics, textbooks, or documents. Different kinds of reading suit different screens.

Do you like to take notes?

Some readers focus purely on reading, while others are built around writing and annotation.

Where is your library?

Consider which ebook stores and services you already use, and which devices work with them.
